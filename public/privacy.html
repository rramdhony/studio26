Data controller: Studio26 (represented by founder Ruben Ramdhony)
Location: Republic of Mauritius
Privacy & data protection contact: hello@studio26.me

We process personal data strictly in accordance with the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017 (Act No. 20 of 2017) and, for international visitors in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What data we collect & lawful basis

We believe the best privacy policy is collecting almost nothing in the first place. This website sets no advertising pixels, no behavioural tracking cookies, and no third-party analytics beacons.

A. Direct email enquiries (live today)

When you write to us at hello@studio26.me, we receive your name, email address, and the project details you choose to share so we can reply to you. The current on-page conversation preview (studio26 / studio assistant) and the interactive brand-template preview on /ai-video run entirely in your browser and send or store no messages.

B. Interactive studio assistant (“Ash”) & online project briefs (when enabled)

When our live AI studio assistant (Ash) or online project brief submission endpoint is enabled on the site, the following safeguards apply:

  • Contact & brief details: We collect your name, email address, optional company name, and project brief only when you explicitly choose to send an enquiry.
  • Lawful basis (DPA 2017 Section 28): Taking pre-contractual steps at your request prior to entering into a service agreement, and our legitimate interest in replying to prospective clients.
  • Voluntary supply (DPA 2017 Section 23): Supplying your contact details is voluntary, though without a name, email address, and project note we cannot reply to an enquiry.
  • Anti-spam rate limiting (when submitting an online brief): We compute a one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of your IP address combined with a private server secret (IP_SALT) and store it alongside your browser’s User-Agent string strictly to cap automated submissions at 5 per hour. We never store your raw IP address in our database.

C. Retention period

Project enquiries that do not proceed to a commercial engagement are reviewed and deleted within 12 months. Where a project proceeds to a contract, records are retained for the statutory accounting and tax period required under Mauritian law.

3. Infrastructure, sub-processors & cross-border transfers

Under Section 36 of the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017, personal data may be processed outside Mauritius where necessary to perform steps requested by you and where appropriate security safeguards are in place:

Provider Purpose Safeguards
Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers & D1) Global edge hosting, media delivery, and encrypted D1 database storage (when online enquiry submission is active). TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

4. Your rights under the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017

Under Part VII (Sections 37 to 41) of the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017, you have the right to:

  • Request access (Section 37) to a copy of the personal data we hold about you free of charge.
  • Request rectification, erasure, or restriction (Section 39) of inaccurate, incomplete, or no-longer-needed personal data.
  • Object to processing (Section 40) in writing at any time, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@studio26.me. In accordance with Section 37(2) of the Act, we respond to all verified requests within one month (extendable by up to two further months where necessary, with written notice).

If you believe a concern has not been resolved satisfactorily, you have the right under Section 6 and Section 37(6) of the Data Protection Act 2017 to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commissioner at the Mauritius Data Protection Office (5th Floor, SICOM Tower, Wall Street, Ebène Cybercity, Mauritius · dpo@govmu.org · dataprotection.govmu.org).

5. Accessibility statement

At Studio26, accessibility is an engineering standard, not an afterthought. Every page we ship is tested against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA across mobile and desktop viewports.

How we build for everyone

  • Keyboard & screen-reader navigation: Every page includes a skip-to-content link, a logical heading hierarchy with a single <h1>, visible focus indicators, and full keyboard operability for dialogs and concept switchers.
  • Motion control & respect for preferences: All hero animations and video loops respect prefers-reduced-motion: reduce automatically and provide explicit on-screen Pause / Play controls (WCAG 2.2.2).
  • Touch targets & contrast: Interactive controls meet a minimum 44×44px tap target on mobile (375px width) and meet WCAG AA contrast ratios (4.5:1 for body copy and 3:1 for large display type).
  • Progressive enhancement: Core navigation, portfolio links, and studio contact details remain readable and functional even if JavaScript or WebGL is unavailable.

Spotted an accessibility barrier on any Studio26 page? Email hello@studio26.me and we will remediate it promptly.
