Privacy

Studio26 is a design studio based in Mauritius. This page explains what happens to the details you send us.

What we collect

  • What you type into the enquiry form: your name, email, company and message.
  • A scrambled (hashed) version of your internet address, used only to stop spam. We cannot turn it back into your address.

Why

To reply to your enquiry and talk about your project. We do not sell your details, add you to mailing lists, or share them with anyone except the services that run this site.

Who else is involved

The site runs on Cloudflare, which also checks that form submissions come from a person (Cloudflare Turnstile). Your enquiry is stored in our Cloudflare database.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 12 months, unless we start working together.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing hello@studio26.me.
