Questions

Are the ads made with AI?

Partly. We use AI for imagery, voice and motion where it helps, then write, edit, typeset and check each ad by hand. We tell you which shots are AI-generated.

Can I use the ads anywhere?

You can run them on your own channels and in paid campaigns, under the usage terms in your agreement: full commercial and paid-media rights across web, social and paid digital channels (Meta, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn and the web) once paid for, with broadcast TV or cinema clearance quoted per campaign. Any stock footage or music we use stays under its own licence, which we choose so that ads are allowed.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, your colours if you have them, the offers you want to promote, and any photos or footage you already own.

How long does the first ad take?

Five to seven working days from the brief to the first ad. After that, new ads arrive on an agreed schedule.

Can I change or pause my plan?

Changes and pauses follow the terms in your agreement: rolling monthly plans: switch tiers, pause or cancel with 7 days’ notice before your next billing cycle.

Do you make longer films?

Yes. Documentaries and explainers are quoted per project and can come with a website built around the film. Ancient Egypt Chronicles shows the format.
