What does Studio26 build?

We design and build custom websites around your brand, with copy, responsive layouts and motion where it serves the experience. You can commission a website on its own or add a dedicated AI assistant for visitor questions and lead capture.

Need campaign creative as well? Our AI video ads and brand films bring your colours, typography and message into social and web campaigns. Website, assistant and video work can be discussed in one project brief.

  • Visual direction and website copy tailored to your business.
  • Layouts built for phones and larger screens, with attention to accessibility.
  • Hosting on Cloudflare's global network.
  • An optional assistant grounded in your approved business information.
  • Custom e-commerce and online stores (including product catalogues, checkout flows and online payment options tailored to your market, scoped per project).
  • Full-service logo design, visual identity, typography and brand guidelines alongside your digital build (quoted on discovery).
  • Custom web applications and iOS/Android mobile app projects scoped and quoted around your workflow.

How much does a website with an AI chatbot cost?

A custom Studio26 website starts at $2,500 USD. A website with a dedicated AI assistant starts at $4,000 USD, plus $95 per month for assistant care and usage.

The monthly plan includes hosting, content updates and up to 1,000 visitor chats. These are starting prices; the final scope is confirmed in your proposal. MUR invoicing is available on request.

Tell us about your pages, content, languages and any booking or business systems you want to connect so we can confirm what your project needs.

How long does a website take?

As a standard guide, a custom Studio26 website typically takes two to four weeks from brief to launch, depending on page count, content readiness and custom features. This is a guide, not a fixed booking. E-commerce, larger applications and dedicated AI assistant integrations are scheduled during discovery.

What are the payment terms?

Standard project terms are a 50% upfront deposit to book and start the project, with the remaining 50% due on final delivery before launch. Assistant care and monthly video plans are billed monthly. Your proposal confirms the scope, schedule and applicable terms.

  • Fixed written quote: Free 30-minute discovery call, followed by a fixed written proposal before any work starts.
  • 50/50 milestone terms: 50% upfront deposit to start, and 50% on final delivery after you review the finished work.
  • 100% client ownership: You own your custom website code, domain and final deliverables upon full payment.
  • One-business-day reply: Direct communication in English or French from Ruben and the studio.

Is SEO included?

Technical SEO foundations include semantic HTML, descriptive page metadata, canonical URLs, a sitemap and appropriate structured data. We can scope ongoing SEO, content strategy and search visibility campaigns separately.

An optional llms.txt file provides a concise site summary. It is not a Google ranking signal. Search rankings, AI recommendations and traffic outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

What can the assistant do?

A dedicated assistant can answer questions about your business and help capture enquiries. Booking and other integrations depend on the agreed project scope. Its answers need approved source material and ongoing updates when your services or prices change.

Can you add an assistant to an existing website?

Yes. Studio26 can also build an assistant for an existing website. The integration and price are confirmed after discovery.

Is Ash a demonstration of a live generative AI model?

Ash, the studio assistant on our site, currently uses approved answers and guided project briefs in English and French. It is a curated site assistant, not a live generative model. A client assistant is scoped separately around the client's needs.
